Render Contexts

  • Vector Rendering w/Canvas
    A very simple example showing the VectorRenderContext and its capabilities. Vector rendering uses lines, points, line and curve paths, and primitive shapes and polygons to generate imagery. This shows using the engine to generate a static image to a Canvas 2d context.
  • Raster Rendering w/Canvas
    This demonstrates the RasterRenderContext and its capabilities. Raster rendering maintains the lines and points of the vector context, but eliminates the primitives and polygons to replace them with Sprites, Tiles, and TileMaps more common in raster-rendered games.
