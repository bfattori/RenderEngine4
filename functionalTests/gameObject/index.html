GameObject

  • Single GameObject (Vector Text)
    This demonstrates the creation of a single GameObject using VectorText and inline text formatting. It shows component part composition and configuration of a Transform2dPart and VectorRendererPart. Assignment of parts to the object, insertion into the GameWorld, and per-frame-driven updates of the object's transformation.
  • 200 GameObjects (Convex polygons)
    Adding more game objects increases the amount of time required for processing. The structure of the engine and rendering pipelines is optimized for speed, shown by this example of 200 convex polygons. Each polygon is between 3 and 12 sides and rotates clockwise or counterclockwise at varying speeds with per-frame-driven updates.
